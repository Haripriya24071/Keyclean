The DSP Under the Hood

📊

Spectral Flux

Measures how fast the frequency spectrum jumps frame-to-frame — clicks cause sudden broadband energy spikes unlike any speech sound.

📈

HF Energy Ratio

Computes the ratio of high-frequency (3kHz+) energy versus total energy. Keystroke transients have far more HF content than voiced speech.

🎯

Rolling MAD Z-score

Uses a robust Median Absolute Deviation baseline — adapts to changing mic gain and room noise without being skewed by the clicks themselves.

🎨

Crossfade Inpainting

Fills detected gaps by time-reversing adjacent audio and blending it in with a raised-cosine crossfade — smooth continuity, no glitch artifacts.
